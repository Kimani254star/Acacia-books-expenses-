About Acacia Expenses

Know what was spent, who approved it and whether it was paid.

Acacia Expenses is where your company logs its spending. Every expense is recorded with its category, supplier and VAT rate, so nothing sits in a receipt pile or a spreadsheet.

Each expense moves through a controlled workflow, from Draft through approval to Paid, so you can see what is waiting for sign-off and what is still outstanding.

Reports and a dashboard turn the entries into spend by category, spend by supplier, paid versus unpaid, and a VAT and input tax summary.

What you get

Everything you need to control spending

Six areas that keep expenses organised from entry to payment.

Log every expense

Record each expense with its description, category, supplier and VAT rate, then filter and search them by text, status or category.

Categories

Group spending into categories you can manage in Settings, so reports show where the money is going.

Approval workflow

Expenses move through statuses such as Draft, Submitted, Pending Approval, Approved, Posted, Paid and Rejected, so you always know where each one stands.

Suppliers

Keep a list of suppliers and see spending by supplier.

Expense reports

Expense by category, expense by supplier, paid versus unpaid, and a VAT and input tax summary.

Dashboard

Total expenses, this month, pending approval, paid, outstanding, VAT and input tax, operating costs and approved, all at a glance.

How it works

From receipt to paid

Three steps, and your spending is under control.

1

Create your account

Enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password. You start with a blank account, ready for your own expenses.

2

Log expenses

Add each expense with its category, supplier and VAT rate, and send it for approval when it is ready.

3

Approve, pay and review

Move approved expenses on to Paid, and use the dashboard and reports to see what was spent and what is outstanding.

Good questions

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you sign up.

How do I create an account?

Choose Create account and enter your company name, your name, your email address and a password of at least 6 characters.

What happens to an expense after I log it?

It moves through a status workflow, for example Draft, Submitted, Pending Approval, Approved, Posted and Paid. An expense can also be Rejected.

Does it handle VAT?

Yes. You choose a VAT rate on each expense, and the reports include a VAT and input tax summary.

Can I see spending by supplier or category?

Yes. The reports show expense by category and expense by supplier, as well as paid versus unpaid.

Can it connect to Acacia Books?

Yes. The Connect page links your account to your Acacia Books company.
